Master American Football

Your complete guide to understanding America's favorite sport

The Basics

📋

The Field

A football field is 100 yards long with 10-yard end zones at each end. The field is marked with yard lines every 5 yards for reference.

⏱️

Game Duration

A game consists of 4 quarters, each 15 minutes long. With timeouts and halftime, a typical game lasts about 3 hours.

🎯

Objective

Score points by moving the ball into the opponent's end zone. Teams alternate between offense (attacking) and defense.

👥

Teams

11 players on the field per side. Teams have an offense, defense, and special teams units that rotate in and out.

Key Positions

Quarterback (QB)

The leader of the offense. Throws passes and calls plays. The most important position on the field.

Running Back (RB)

Carries the football and catches short passes. Creates running plays and contributes to pass defense.

Wide Receiver (WR)

Catches passes downfield. Must have great speed, agility, and hands to separate from defenders.

Offensive Line

Protects the QB and creates running lanes. Center, Guards, and Tackles work as a unit.

Linebacker (LB)

Middle of the defense. Tackles runners and covers receivers. Often the defensive leader.

Defensive Back (DB)

Covers receivers and defends passes. Safety and Cornerbacks make up the secondary.

How to Score

6

Touchdown

Moving the ball into the opponent's end zone. Can be achieved by running or catching a pass.

3

Field Goal

Kicking the ball through the uprights from anywhere on the field (usually 20-50 yards away).

2

Extra Point

After a touchdown, score 2 points by getting the ball into the end zone from the 2-yard line.

1

Point After

After a touchdown, kick the ball through the uprights for 1 point (extra point conversion).

Game Strategies

🔓 Pass Plays

The quarterback throws the ball to receivers downfield. Effective for quick scores and covering long distances. Requires timing and accuracy.

🏃 Run Plays

The running back carries the ball. Controlled and reliable. Wears down the defense and eats up time on the clock.

🛡️ Defensive Formations

Defenses line up in different formations to stop runs and passes. Common formations are 3-4, 4-3, and nickel packages.

⏰ Clock Management

Teams manage the play clock strategically. Running the ball slows time, while passing can stop the clock with incomplete throws.
